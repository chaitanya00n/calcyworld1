Concrete Wall & Stairs Calculator

Free

More about this tool

Concrete for a wall or a flight of steps. It is free, works on any device and your numbers never leave your browser.

How to use the Concrete Wall & Stairs Calculator

  1. Enter or choose the dimensions in.
  2. Enter or choose the thickness / depth in.
  3. Enter or choose the structure.
  4. Enter or choose the length / width.
  5. Enter or choose the height / total rise.
  6. Enter or choose the thickness / tread depth.
  7. Your result updates instantly — use Copy, Share, Save image or Print to keep it.

Worked example

For example, with length / width 6, height / total rise 2.4 and thickness / tread depth 20, the concrete needed comes to 3.024 m³.

Formula

Wall V = length × height × thickness.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Concrete Wall & Stairs Calculator free?

Yes. Every calculator on this site is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you type are not sent to our server.

Does the Concrete Wall & Stairs Calculator work in my country and currency?

Yes. Your country is detected automatically and amounts are shown in your currency and number format. You can change the country at any time from the menu.
