Frequently asked questions

How often are rates updated?

Rates refresh automatically from a reference data feed, usually daily. The time of the last update is shown under the result.

Is this the rate my bank will give me?

No. These are mid-market reference rates. Banks and money transfer services add a margin or fee, so the rate you receive is usually a little worse.

Is the ILS to USD Converter (Israeli Shekel to US Dollar) free?

Yes. Every calculator on this site is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you type are not sent to our server.

Does the ILS to USD Converter (Israeli Shekel to US Dollar) work in my country and currency?

Yes. Your country is detected automatically and amounts are shown in your currency and number format. You can change the country at any time from the menu.
