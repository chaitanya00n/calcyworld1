Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy describes what Calcyworld does with information when you use the website. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

The short version

  • The numbers you type into a calculator stay in your browser.
  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We keep a simple count of how often each tool is opened in each country. The count holds no personal data.
  • Advertising, if it is switched on, can use cookies from our advertising partners. Details are below.

Information that stays on your device

Calculator inputs and results are processed by your browser and are not sent to us. Your preferences are saved in your browser’s local storage: day or night mode, language, country and currency, recently used tools, and a cached copy of the tool list that makes search fast. This is not a cookie, we cannot read it, and you can clear it from your browser settings at any time.

Page-view counter

To show which calculators are popular in each country, the site records one count for a tool, the two-letter country code your browser reports, and the date. No IP address, cookie or other identifier is stored with that count. To avoid counting a refresh twice, the server keeps a one-way hash of your IP address and the tool for up to 20 minutes and then discards it. Visitors whose browsers send Do Not Track are not counted.

Server logs

Like almost every website, the web server that hosts this site may keep standard access logs, which include your IP address, the page requested and your browser type. Hosting providers use these logs for security and troubleshooting and delete them on their own schedule.

Fonts

By default the theme loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. When it does, your browser requests the font files from Google, and Google receives your IP address and browser details. The site owner can turn this off in Appearance, Customize, Calcyworld, Colours & style, and the site then uses fonts already installed on your device.

Exchange rates and crypto prices

Prices are fetched by our own server from public feeds and passed to your browser. Your visit is not sent to those feeds.

Advertising

If advertising is shown on this site, our advertising partners, such as Google AdSense, may place or read cookies in your browser to show ads and measure them. Where the law requires consent, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, ads that use cookies are shown only after you agree. You can manage personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com or optout.aboutads.info.

Comments

If comments are open on a blog article and you leave one, WordPress stores the name, email address, website, IP address and text you submit. A comment appears publicly with your name; your email address does not.

Cookies

The site itself sets no tracking cookies. WordPress sets a small cookie when an administrator signs in or when a visitor chooses to remember their details in the comment form. Cookies from advertising partners are covered above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the EU and UK GDPR or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to see, correct or delete personal data held about you, to object to its use, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Since we hold almost no personal data, the usual case is a comment you left on a blog article. Ask the site owner and it will be dealt with.

Children

The site is general-purpose and not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
