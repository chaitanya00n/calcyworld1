      What is Calcyworld?

      Calcyworld is a free website with more than 2,300 online calculators and unit converters. Type your numbers and the answer appears straight away. There is nothing to install and no account to create, and it works the same on a phone, a tablet, a laptop and a TV browser.

      Calculators for money, tax and property

      The finance tools include EMI and loan calculators, mortgage and home-loan tools, SIP, fixed deposit and compound interest calculators, and retirement planners. Tax & Salary has income tax, GST and VAT, and take-home pay calculators for several countries. Every tool page shows the formula it uses and a worked example, so you can check the answer yourself.

      Health, fitness and everyday calculators

      BMI, calorie and body-fat calculators sit next to age, date and time tools, percentage and fraction calculators, grade and GPA tools, cooking conversions and fuel-cost estimators. If you are not sure where to look, type what you want into the search box and the right tool opens.

      Unit and currency converters

      About 800 converters cover length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed, energy, data size and more. The currency converter uses rates for around 160 currencies, refreshed twice a day, and crypto prices from CoinGecko.

      In your country and your language

      Calcyworld picks the currency, number format and units that suit your country and lists the tools people in that country use most. Choose another country in the header and the language follows. Ten languages are available, including Hindi, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Indonesian, Bengali and Russian.

      Frequently asked questions

      Are the calculators free?

      Yes. Every calculator and converter is free, needs no sign-up, and shows its result as you type.

      Are my numbers stored or sent anywhere?

      No. Calculations run in your browser and the numbers you type stay there. The privacy policy lists the few things the site does record, such as a count of how often each tool is opened in each country.

      How accurate are the results?

      The formulas are the standard ones for each topic, and each tool page shows its formula and a worked example. Results are estimates: a lender, tax office or doctor has the final word on anything that matters.

      Where do the exchange rates come from?

      From the open data feed of Exchange Rate API, refreshed twice a day. They are mid-market rates, so the rate your bank gives will differ slightly.

      Can I use Calcyworld in my own language?

      Yes. There are ten interface languages. The site switches language when you pick a country, and you can choose a different language at any time from the header.
